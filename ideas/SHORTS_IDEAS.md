# 30 Shorts ideas for Japa or Stay

Six categories, five ideas each, every one built from the six environments the studio already draws
(Lagos street, London street/bus stop, UK apartment, airport, Nigerian home, UK workplace) and the five recurring
characters (Chinedu, Amaka, Tunde, Ada, Mama Chinedu). Every idea ends on an A/B choice for the comments and has a Part 2.
Money figures in any episode are fictional game values and must be labelled that way on screen.

## Scoring rubric (out of 25)

| Score | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Hook** (1–5) | Does the first line land a surprise or a stake within 3 seconds? |
| **Relate** (1–5) | Will a Nigerian at home or abroad say "na me be this"? |
| **Debate** (1–5) | Is A vs B genuinely split, so people argue in the comments? |
| **Series** (1–5) | Does it set up a Part 2 people will follow for? |
| **Build** (1–5) | How easily it's made with the existing sets, cast and UI (5 = no new art) |
